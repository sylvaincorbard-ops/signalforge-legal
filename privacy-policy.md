PRIVACY POLICY — ANTENNA ALIGN PRO
Last updated: June 2026
Publisher: SignalForge — fieldsignalstudio@gmail.com

1. DATA COLLECTED
Antenna Align Pro only collects data necessary for the app 
to function:

- GPS Location: used for antenna pointing and tower map. 
  Never shared with third parties.
- Network data (RSRP, RSRQ, SINR, PCI): local measurements 
  stored only on your device.
- Tour history: stored locally, exportable by the user as CSV.

2. DATA NOT COLLECTED
We do not collect:
- Personal data (name, email, phone number)
- Browsing or in-app behavior data
- Contacts, photos or other device data

3. IN-APP PURCHASES
Purchases are handled by Apple App Store or Google Play Store 
and RevenueCat. We do not store any payment or banking data.

4. DATA SHARING
No personal data is sold or shared with third parties 
for advertising purposes.

Third-party services used:
- Google Maps: map display (Google privacy policy)
- RevenueCat: subscription management (RevenueCat policy)

5. STORAGE
All data is stored locally on your device.
No external server is used to store your data.

6. YOUR RIGHTS
You can delete all data by uninstalling the application.

For any questions: fieldsignalstudio@gmail.com

8. UPDATES
This policy may be updated. The last update date is shown
